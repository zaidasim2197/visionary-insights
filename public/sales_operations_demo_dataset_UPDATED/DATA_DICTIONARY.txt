============================================================
INVENTORY BUSINESS RULES
============================================================

quantity_available =
quantity_on_hand - quantity_reserved

In Stock:
quantity_available > reorder_level

Low Stock:
0 < quantity_available <= reorder_level

Out of Stock:
quantity_available = 0

Inventory Discrepancy:
quantity_available < 0

The dataset intentionally contains only a small number of
negative-stock records for exception/validation testing.


============================================================
RECEIVABLE BUSINESS RULES
============================================================

outstanding_amount =
invoice_amount - amount_paid

Paid:
outstanding_amount = 0

Current:
outstanding balance exists and invoice is not due within
the next 14 days.

Due Soon:
outstanding balance exists and due date is within 14 days.

Overdue:
due_date < reference_date AND outstanding_amount > 0.

Payment records reconcile exactly with amount_paid.


============================================================
DELIVERY PERFORMANCE
============================================================

On Time:
delivered_date <= required_date

Delayed:
delivered_date > required_date

delivery_delay_days:
MAX(delivered_date - required_date, 0)

delivery_lead_time_days:
delivered_date - order_date

Overdue Open Order:
order has not been delivered and required_date has passed.


============================================================
IMPORTANT DASHBOARD RULES
============================================================

1. Cancelled orders must not contribute to sales.

2. Returns reduce net sales.

3. Every dashboard total must be calculated from source records.

4. Customer rankings derive from qualifying sales orders.

5. Product rankings derive from qualifying order items.

6. Inventory value uses quantity_on_hand × unit_cost.

7. Receivables and payments must always reconcile.

8. Operational dates never exceed the dataset reference date.
